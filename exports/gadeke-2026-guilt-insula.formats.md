## What MIRA drops

## What MIRA flattens

`tests`, `confirms`, `validates`, `extends` and `replicates` all become `mira:supports`; `contradicts`, `rules-out` and `dissociates-with` all become `mira:opposes`. The relation survives; the reason it was drawn does not.
