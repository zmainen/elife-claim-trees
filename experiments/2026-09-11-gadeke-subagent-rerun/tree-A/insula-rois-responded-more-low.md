**Notes from extraction:** Canonical phrasing follows the Caption-reader, which is more precise and avoids the prose's mentalistic framing ('situations during which participants experienced guilt') — the design measures happiness and BOLD, not felt guilt. Two caveats to carry to review: the Results-reader notes the insulae were selected by a two-condition screen (higher Social than Partner activity plus a significant Social:LowOutcome interaction), so the ROI analysis is partly selection-dependent; and the Structure-reader's voxels-as-observations flag applies to the significance of these LMM coefficients. Coefficients are in Appendix 1—table 6.

**results-reader evidence:**
> Thus, activation in our insula ROIs increased in situations during which participants experienced guilt for low outcomes impacting their partner, compared to similar outcomes resulting from the partner's choices.

**caption-reader evidence:**
> ( E ) Coefficients of LMMs indicated that insula ROIs (see D) mirrored the guilt effect observed in our behavioural data: voxels here responded more to low lottery outcomes (L) for the partner when these resulted from participant's rather than the partner's choices, even when responses to high outcomes were subtracted (L–H). All coefficients and differences reported are significantly different from 0 (see  Appendix 1—table 6 ).
