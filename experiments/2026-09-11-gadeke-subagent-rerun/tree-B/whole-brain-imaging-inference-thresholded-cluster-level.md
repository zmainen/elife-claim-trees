**Notes from extraction:** The two readers reach the same threshold statement from different places (Figure 4 caption and methods), which is why this is two-source. A discrepancy worth review: the caption's blanket assertion that 'all results shown' survive correction omits the methods' disjunction 'across the whole brain or a smaller volume', and the fig4f insula cluster in that same figure is in fact significant only after small-volume correction. The correction volume must be checked per cluster; the methods do not state which contrasts used small-volume correction or how those volumes were defined.
