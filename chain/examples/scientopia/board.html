What this is

This is one research project — aurora, juniper, larch, library, meridian — carried through the whole research → publishing → funding cycle by a mix of people, models and scripts. Every step is a question put to the project, and every answer is a set of plain claims — a hypothesis, a method, a result, a verdict. Nothing on this page was written by hand: it is all read back from the record of what was asked and answered.
