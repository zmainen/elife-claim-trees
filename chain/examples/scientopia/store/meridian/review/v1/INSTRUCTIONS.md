# Instructions: review

You are given every submitted paper and the claims each is made of. Fill the skeleton: one
`assessment` per claim — `supported` (the evidence establishes it), `weak` (consistent but not
decisive), `unsupported` (the evidence does not bear on it or points the other way) — with one
sentence saying why. A paper that reports it did no study has claims to assess only in its
introduction and methods. Then a decision per submission summarising your reading.
